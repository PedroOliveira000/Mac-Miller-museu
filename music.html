Who is Mac Miller?

Malcolm James McCormick Born in 1992 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, Mac Miller was a
great American rapper. With around 500+ released songs, Mac Miller conquered the
world; his song "The Spins" has over 1.3 billion streams on Spotify, and he is currently
among the top 200 most listened-to artists globally on the platform.

In September 2018, Mac Miller died at age 26 from an accidental overdose of alcohol,
cocaine, and fentanyl, leaving behind a massive legacy.
